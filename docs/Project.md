- **Milestone funding:** Created is not the same as funded. Verify funds before starting paid work.

- **Refund policy:** Client mentioned 10- or 14-day money-back guarantee. Determine whether commission can be clawed back or delayed.
- **Legal outreach:** Do not assume automated Google Maps scraping, bulk email, calls, or SMS are universally permitted. Respect source terms, privacy laws, CAN-SPAM, TCPA, do-not-call rules, consent/opt-out requirements, and applicable local laws.
- **Access/security:** Use least-privilege GHL user access, MFA where supported, no shared personal credentials, and authorized use of customer data.
- **Sales promises:** Do not guarantee meeting or customer conversion rates; client is responsible for closing.
- **Support:** Define limits on landing-page work, revisions, custom integrations, post-onboarding support, and Loom production.
- **Verification:** No direct evidence yet of client's business identity, pricing, live demo, existing templates, or sales conversion performance. No evidence of fraud either.
